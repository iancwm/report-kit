# Investment view

Nexa Cloud Solutions remains an attractive way to participate in enterprise AI adoption. Our channel work points to slower deployment timelines, but usage-based orchestration continues to grow as customers consolidate workloads rather than abandon them.

## Demand is slowing, not breaking

We trim infrastructure growth assumptions by one to two quarters while keeping our price target at 245. Recurring platform revenue and services mix provide an offset to lower-margin hardware-linked activity.

[[REPORTKIT-VISUAL:fig:usage-index]]

## Mix and margins do the work

AI orchestration and token routing rise from 21 percent of revenue in FY25A to 28 percent in FY27E. The resulting mix shift supports gross-margin expansion even with a more conservative infrastructure outlook.

[[REPORTKIT-VISUAL:fig:platform-mix]]

## Valuation and risks

Our base case is 245 per share, with a bear case of 135 and a bull case of 310. The principal debate is duration, not direction: a slower but longer enterprise adoption cycle remains the most likely path.

[[REPORTKIT-VISUAL:fig:risk-reward]]

The estimate revision is concentrated in lower-margin services activity. We therefore expect improving platform mix and operating leverage to matter more than near-term unit growth.
